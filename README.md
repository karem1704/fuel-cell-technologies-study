# fuel-cell-technologies-study
Technical study of fuel cell technologies, electrochemical principles, energy efficiency and hydrogen applications
# Fuel Cell Technologies: Principles, Performance and Applications

**Academic Project | Industrial Chemistry Engineering | INSAT, Tunisia | 2025–2026**

## Overview

This academic project explores fuel cell technologies as electrochemical systems for converting the chemical energy of a fuel into electricity and heat.

The study examines the operating principles, components, performance, limitations and applications of fuel cells, with a particular focus on Proton Exchange Membrane Fuel Cells (PEMFCs) and Solid Oxide Fuel Cells (SOFCs).

## Objectives

- Understand the electrochemical principles of fuel cell operation.
- Analyze the main components and architecture of fuel cell systems.
- Compare the main fuel cell technologies and their operating conditions.
- Examine thermodynamic efficiency, energy balances and polarization losses.
- Explore thermal management and combined heat and power (CHP).
- Investigate hydrogen production, storage and potential applications in the energy transition.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Electrochemical Fundamentals
- Anode, cathode and electrolyte
- Hydrogen oxidation and oxygen reduction
- Gibbs free energy and theoretical cell voltage
- Activation, ohmic and concentration losses

### 2. Fuel Cell Technologies
- Proton Exchange Membrane Fuel Cells (PEMFC)
- Solid Oxide Fuel Cells (SOFC)
- Alkaline Fuel Cells (AFC)
- Molten Carbonate Fuel Cells (MCFC)
- Phosphoric Acid Fuel Cells (PAFC)

### 3. Energy Performance
- Thermodynamic and electrical efficiency
- Energy and heat balances
- Comparison with conventional energy conversion systems
- Combined heat and power applications

### 4. Thermal Management
- Air and liquid cooling systems
- Heat recovery
- Thermal control and system durability

### 5. Hydrogen and Applications
- Hydrogen production pathways
- Hydrogen storage and transportation
- Transportation and stationary power generation
- Integration with renewable energy systems

## Engineering Relevance

This study develops a foundation for understanding electrochemical energy conversion, process performance, thermal management and the role of hydrogen technologies in sustainable industrial systems.

## Project Deliverable

The main deliverable is an academic technical report presenting the literature review, engineering principles, comparative analyses and technological perspectives of fuel cell systems.
